## IEEE rule on AI-generated content

IEEE requires authors to disclose the use of AI-generated content (text, figures, images, code) in the **Acknowledgment** section of the paper. The disclosure names the AI system, identifies the sections that contain AI-generated content, and briefly states the level of use. Using AI only for editing and grammar falls outside the rule, but disclosure is still recommended.

The plan reserves space for this in the Acknowledgment. Check the wording against IEEE's current submission policies before submission.
