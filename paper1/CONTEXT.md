# Paper 1: start here

Written by the planning session on 2026-11-16, when the PI started drafting with the compute node. Everything the draft needs is in this repository, and this file says where.

**The paper.** An off-the-shelf tabular foundation model (TabPFN v2, TabICLv2), pretrained on synthetic tables only, used as a Wi-Fi fingerprint localizer with no training: the surveyed fingerprints of a building are its context, a new scan is the query. Compared with six tuned classical baselines on SODIndoorLoc (three buildings), with two stress tests: fewer surveyed reference points, and access points that go missing after the survey. Target: IEEE PerCom 2027 Work-in-Progress, 4 pages including references.

## Facts the draft must state

- **Test points and reference points share locations in one building.** In CETC331, all 84 test points sit at reference-point locations (different scans, same places). In HCXY none of 86 do; in SYL, 1 of 102. Source: `results/P1-E01/metrics/e1_shared_locations.csv`. Report per building, not only the average.
- **TabPFN context cap on HCXY.** TabPFN v2 sees 26 of the 30 scans per reference point there (9,854 rows) to stay under its 10,000-row limit. `P1-E03` ran past the limit on purpose and says so.
- **The phone and user breakdown is not a cross-device test.** Every test phone and user also appears in training (`P1-E03`).
- **Published numbers of other papers** (Locaris, IndoorCART, CR-AP) are quoted as reported, in separate rows or sentences, marked as a different protocol. Their metric definitions differ: `unverified.md` §B.
- **SODIndoorLoc citation.** The dataset README asks for a citation of Bi et al., Satellite Navigation 3:25 (2022).
- **Disclosure of AI use** goes in the Acknowledgment (`context/VENUE.md`).

## Working with the literature (planning-session guidance)

- Treat the review as a pool. §5 of `review.md` suggests a shortlist of 16; §4 lists the numbers we may need to quote, with their definitions.
- Cite only keys in `refs.bib`. Next to each sentence about prior work, keep a LaTeX comment naming its evidence row, for example `% P004:E023`, so the claim can be traced.
- Take numbers from other papers only from sources read in full (`access_level` FULLTEXT in `sources/index.csv`). The 22 sources read at abstract level are listed in `unverified.md` §E.
- Write the novelty claim as "to our knowledge". One planned search (papers citing TabPFN) could not be run: `unverified.md` §A.

## Not to use

- Any number from the informal pilot run in the planning sandbox before the experiments. It is not in this repository and must not appear in the paper.
- TabPFN 2.5 or later. Their licence excludes research relating to surveillance.
