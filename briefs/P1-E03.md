## Questions

- **A.** Are the predictive distributions of the tabular foundation models calibrated, and does their predicted spread tell which fixes are bad?
- **B.** How many scans per reference point does the context need, what does the context size cost in time and GPU memory, and does the 10,000-row cap on HCXY cost accuracy?
- **C.** How stable are the full-survey results of the tabular foundation models over 20 seeds instead of 5?
- **D.** Are the missing-access-point curves of `P1-E02` Part B stable with 30 draws instead of 10?
- **E.** Does the error differ between the phones and the users of the test rows?

## Setup

Everything not stated here is as in `P1-E01` and `P1-E02`: data, the two ways of writing "not detected", model definitions, best values, metrics. No model is tuned in this brief.

### A. Uncertainty

- Configurations: all five. Models: `tabpfn-nan`, `tabpfn-ph`, `tabicl` (seeds 0 to 4) and `gp` (one run).
- Fit on the whole training file (TabPFN context capped on HCXY exactly as in `P1-E01`).
- Quantile levels 0.025, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.95, 0.975 per coordinate: TabPFN `predict(output_type="main", quantiles=...)`; TabICL `predict(output_type=["mean", "quantiles"], alphas=...)`; GP: Gaussian quantiles from `predict(return_std=True)`.
- Point prediction: the mean, as in `P1-E01`.
- Metrics: empirical coverage of the central 50, 80, 90 and 95% intervals per coordinate and for both coordinates at once; mean interval width; Spearman correlation between the predicted spread (mean of the two 80% widths) and the positioning error; mean error of the 10% most and least confident test rows.

### B. Scans per reference point

- Configurations: HCXY-All and SYL-All (30 scans per reference point). CETC331 has one scan per point and is not used.
- Context: k scans of every reference point, drawn without replacement with seed s, for k = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 26, 30 and s = 0 to 4.
- Models: `tabpfn-nan`, `tabpfn-ph`, `tabicl` (seed s), `knn` with its `P1-E01` best values and `gp` (seed-0 draw only; neither has randomness).
- TabPFN uses the whole k-scan context. Above 10,000 rows (HCXY at k = 30: 11,370 rows) it runs with `ignore_pretraining_limits=True`; k = 26 reproduces the `P1-E01` cap (9,854 rows).
- Metrics: the five error statistics, fit and predict seconds, peak GPU memory.

### C. More seeds

- `tabpfn-nan`, `tabpfn-ph`, `tabicl`, all five configurations, seeds 5 to 19, everything else exactly as in `P1-E01` step 11.

### D. More Part B draws

- `P1-E02` Part B with draws 10 to 29 (shares 10 to 60%), all ten models, the three configurations. Models fitted once with seed 0 and the `P1-E01` best values.

### E. Phone and user

- No new runs. From the saved per-row predictions of `P1-E01` (seeds 0 to 4) and `P1-E03` C, the mean error per test-row `PhoneID` and `UserID`, for HCXY-All and SYL-All (CETC331 has one phone and one user).
